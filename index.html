A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Can you help before we have a detailed specification?

Yes. A discovery engagement can clarify the problem, explore options, and define a scope before implementation.

Can you work with an existing product or team?

We can assess an existing system, deliver a focused improvement, or collaborate with your engineers on a defined scope.

How are costs and timelines agreed?

We discuss goals, constraints, dependencies, and scope before proposing an engagement. Estimates and milestones are agreed for the specific project.

What happens after launch?

Handover includes agreed documentation and operational guidance. Ongoing engineering support can be included as a separate engagement.
